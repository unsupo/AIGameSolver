POKEMON RED WALKTHROUGH SNIPPETS

PALLET TOWN:
- You start in your bedroom. 
- Go downstairs and talk to Mom.
- Go outside and try to leave town through the tall grass north.
- Professor Oak will stop you and take you to his lab.
- Choose your starter: Bulbasaur (Grass), Charmander (Fire), or Squirtle (Water).
- Fight your rival Blue.
- Go north to Viridian City.

VIRIDIAN CITY:
- Go to the Poke Mart. The clerk will give you Oak's Parcel.
- Take the Parcel back to Professor Oak in Pallet Town.
- He will give you a Pokedex and 5 Poke Balls.
- Go back to Viridian City and head west to Route 22 if you want to fight Blue again, or north to Route 2.

VIRIDIAN FOREST:
- Catch Pikachu here if you are lucky.
- Navigate the maze to reach Pewter City.
- Train your Pokemon to at least Level 10-12 before fighting Brock.

PEWTER CITY:
- The first Gym Leader is Brock. He uses Rock-type Pokemon (Geodude and Onix).
- Grass and Water moves are super effective.
- Defeat him to get the Boulder Badge.
- Head east toward Mt. Moon.

MT. MOON:
- Buy a Magikarp from the man in the Poke Center if you want a future Gyarados.
- Find the Moon Stone inside the cave.
- Choose between the Dome Fossil and Helix Fossil at the end.
- Exit east to Cerulean City.

CERULEAN CITY:
- Defeat Misty at the Gym. She uses Water-type Pokemon (Staryu and Starmie).
- Electric and Grass moves are best.
- Defeat her to get the Cascade Badge.
- Go north to Nugget Bridge and visit Bill at his sea cottage to get the S.S. Ticket.
